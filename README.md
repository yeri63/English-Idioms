# English Idioms
<div align="center">

<p>English idioms are non-literal, everyday phrases whose figurative meaning cannot be guessed just by looking at the definitions of the individual words.</p>
<p>Native speakers use idioms constantly in daily conversation, film, TV, social media, etc. Missing them can lead to confusion or misunderstanding literal translations. Recognizing and using common expressions helps you connect easily with native speakers and feel more at home in English-speaking environments.</p>
<p>Here are a few examples...</p>
<div align="center">

Idiom | Meaning
:-- | :--
A dime a dozen. | Anything that is common, inexpensive, and easy to get or available anywhere.
A hot potato. | A controversial issue or situation that is awkward or unpleasant to deal with.
Ace in the hole. | A hidden or secret strength; an unrevealed advantage.
</div>
